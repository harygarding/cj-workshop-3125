Stage 1 of 6

👁️ Visibility

Where and how do candidates discover you as an employer?

Opening prompt →

"Think about the last person you hired. How did they first find out you were looking for someone?"

Q

Which job boards or platforms does your store currently post on — and why those ones?

Q

Do you ever ask new starters how they first heard about your vacancy?

Q

How complete is your Indeed employer profile — photos, description, company info?

Q

Do you use any offline methods: window signs, social media, employee referrals?

Q

If a candidate searches "jobs near me" in your town tonight, will your listing appear?

Q

Do you have a consistent process for when and how you post new vacancies?

Stage 2 of 6

🏆 Brand Appeal

Once a candidate notices you, what makes them curious enough to find out more?

Opening prompt →

"A 19-year-old is scrolling Indeed and sees your listing next to five others — what makes them click on yours?"

Q

What do your current job ads say about working for you — beyond just the role description?

Q

Do your listings include real photos of your team, store, or actual work environment?

Q

Have you checked your Indeed employer reviews lately — and have you responded to any?

Q

What is your genuine #1 selling point as a local employer right now?

Q

Does a candidate reading your listing know who they're actually applying to work for?

Q

If you lined up your ad alongside a competitor's, what would make a candidate choose yours?

Live Activity · Brand Appeal

What Does AI Say About Your Store?

Job seekers are increasingly using AI to research employers before they even read a job ad. In 30 seconds, you can find out exactly what AI already knows — and says — about working for you.

📱
Step 1 — Take out your phone

Open ChatGPT, Perplexity, or do a Google search and look at the AI Overview at the top.

🔍
Step 2 — Ask the question

Type: "What's it like to work at [your company name]?" or "Is [company] a good employer?"

🗣️
Step 3 — Read it aloud

Share what comes back with the group. Is it accurate? Positive? Would it encourage someone to apply?

Q

Was what came back accurate — and would it encourage or put off a candidate?

Q

Where do you think the AI learned that information about your company?

Q

Did your job ads, Indeed reviews, or employer profile shape the answer?

Q

What would you want AI to say about working for you?

💡

AI tools learn from job boards, review platforms, and public content about your company. Your Indeed profile, job descriptions, and employer reviews directly shape what AI says about your store — and what candidates see before they ever click Apply.

Stage 3 of 6

💰 The Offer

What specifically convinces a candidate that applying is worth their time?

Opening prompt →

"If a candidate is choosing between you and two other local employers — what's the one thing that tips them towards you?"

✅ Strong EVP — what good looks like

"Paid weekly · free food every shift · hours built around your schedule · team leader pathway within 6 months"

❌ Weak EVP — what most stores say

"You'll receive a Domino's uniform and access to the employee discount scheme"

Q

Is your hourly rate clearly shown in the ad — and how does it compare to local competitors?

Q

Do you list all relevant benefits: free food, flexible shifts, weekly pay, uniform provided?

Q

Are hours, contracted shifts, and scheduling requirements clear before someone applies?

Q

Do you mention training, development, or any progression pathway in the listing?

Q

Is there anything in your listing that speaks to a candidate's life — flexibility for college, weekends off?

Q

Would a 20-year-old reading your job ad feel genuinely excited, or just neutral?

Stage 4 of 6

📋 Getting Applications

How easy and frictionless is it for someone to actually apply?

📱 Indeed data: 87% of hospitality job searches happen on a mobile phone — is your apply process ready for that?
Opening prompt →

"Have you ever tried applying to your own job listing on a mobile phone, from start to finish?"

Q

How many steps does your application process involve from clicking "Apply" to submitting?

Q

Have you personally tried applying to your own listing on a mobile phone recently?

Q

Do you ask for a full CV for entry-level crew roles — and is that really necessary?

Q

Are there friction points: mandatory account creation, long forms, or eligibility tests?

Q

Does a candidate know upfront what information they'll need before they start applying?

Q

Could a motivated person complete your application in under 3 minutes on their phone?

Stage 5 of 6

⚡ Responding & Screening

What happens after someone hits apply — and how quickly does it happen?

⏱️ Candidates who don't hear back within 24 hours are 3× more likely to accept a competitor's offer first
Opening prompt →

"Think about the last candidate who didn't show for an interview. When did you first contact them after they applied?"

Q

What is your typical response time after a new application arrives — hours or days?

Q

Does every applicant receive an automatic acknowledgement the moment they apply?

Q

Who in your store owns the job of reviewing and responding to new applications?

Q

What does your interview invite process look like — phone call, text message, or email?

Q

Do you send a reminder message to candidates the day before their interview slot?

Q

What is your interview no-show rate — and what's your honest view on what's causing it?

Stage 6 of 6

🎉 Making the Hire

What happens at the point of offer — and does it set the relationship up for success?

Opening prompt →

"How many new starters don't come back after their first or second shift — and what do you think is driving that?"

Q

How do you deliver a job offer — phone call, text, email — and how quickly after interview?

Q

Is all onboarding paperwork completed before the new starter's first shift?

Q

Does a new hire know exactly what to expect on day one before they even arrive?

Q

Do you have a buddy or welcome system to support people through their first week?

Q

What happens if a new team member has a difficult or confusing first shift?

Q

How many new starters leave within the first four weeks — and what is the main reason?
